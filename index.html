📋 Hospital Policy

Diagnosis Termination100%
Above 100%, a fully-diagnosed patient is kept for extra paid tests they don't actually need - quiet-time cash, at the cost of extra strain and the risk they get fed up and leave without paying at all.
Send Staff to Rest50%
Low: staff rest early and often - alert, but less time actually working. High: staff push through exhaustion - more mistakes, unhappier, and they may resign if pushed too far.
Staff Leave Rooms
Off: staff stay in their own room (most efficient, but you need one for every room you want staffed). On: idle staff can be pulled to help wherever the queue is worst - fewer staff needed overall, at the cost of constant rotation between rooms.
